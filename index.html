About Me

I am a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, supervised by Prof. Trapti Jain and Prof. Neminath Hubballi. My doctoral thesis is Detection and Mitigation of Cyber Attacks in Smart Grids using SCADA and Synchrophasor Measurements.

My research keeps power-grid monitoring reliable under false data injection (FDI) and denial-of-service (DoS) attacks. It combines statistical detection, machine learning and power-system state estimation. I work with both SCADA/RTU and PMU measurements, using the two data sources together for attack detection and hybrid state estimation. A key part of the work is telling malicious measurement manipulation apart from legitimate grid events.

I have experience with RTDS-based hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testbeds, hardware and virtual PMUs, and field synchrophasor data from a 765/400 kV transmission network. I am first author of papers in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics and IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications, and was a research intern at West Virginia University, USA, in 2025.

Research Interests

01

Wide-Area Monitoring Security

Cyber-physical security of synchrophasor (PMU) and SCADA-based wide-area monitoring systems.

02

Measurement Integrity

Integrity of synchronised measurements, including time-synchronisation attacks.

03

ML-Based Attack Detection

Statistical and deep-learning detectors that separate FDI attacks and anomalies from legitimate grid events.

04

Attack Impact on State Estimation

How attacks affect static (WLS) and dynamic (cubature Kalman filter) state estimation and system operation.

05

Resilience & Measurement Recovery

Real-time prediction of missing synchrophasor channels during DoS attacks and data unavailability.
