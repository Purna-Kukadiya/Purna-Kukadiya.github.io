Research

Detecting and mitigating cyber attacks on smart-grid monitoring using SCADA and synchrophasor measurements.

Doctoral Research Contributions

Statistical and Measurement-Aware Detection of FDI Attacks

False Data InjectionWeibull Distribution SCADA + PMUEvent Classification
  • Developed a topology-independent statistical detector for FDI attacks, based on the Weibull distribution and using SCADA and PMU measurements. It also detects attacks designed to evade conventional bad-data detection.
  • Extended the approach to a two-stage framework that combines measurement-aware features with spatial classification to tell FDI attacks apart from legitimate power-system events.
  • Evaluated detection across varying attack magnitudes using RTDS-generated IEEE 14-bus measurements.
Output: IEEE PES General Meeting 2024 [C4].

Transformer-Autoencoder Anomaly Detection for Synchrophasor Data

  • Developed a reconstruction-based Transformer autoencoder that detects anomalies in short PMU time-series windows, even when the training data contains both normal and anomalous measurements.
  • Evaluated the model on cyber-physical HIL synchrophasor measurements.
  • Co-developed an open-source synchrophasor dataset of cyber, physical and combined disturbances on the WVU RTDS testbed. The coauthored paper received the SGSMA 2026 Second Best Paper Award.
Outputs: IEEE ETFG 2025 [C2]; SGSMA 2026 [C1].

Measurement Recovery for Hybrid State Estimation under DoS Attacks

Denial of ServiceCNN-LSTM WLSCubature Kalman Filter
23 / 25PMU-loss scenarios with lower SE error
  • Studied how sustained, DoS-related PMU data loss affects weighted least-squares (WLS) and cubature Kalman filter (CKF) hybrid state estimation.
  • Developed a CNN-LSTM model that uses PMU and RTU measurements to predict missing synchrophasor channels, without training a separate model for each affected channel.
  • Achieved lower state-estimation error than the compared methods in 23 of 25 evaluated PMU-loss scenarios.
Output: IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 2024 [J2].

  • Developed an enhanced TSMixer with reversible instance normalisation to predict synchrophasor measurements while data is unavailable.
  • Evaluated it on field measurements from 37 PMUs on a 765/400 kV network, and on an IEEE 39-bus RTDS HIL testbed with SEL hardware PMUs.
  • Assessed the effect of the predictions on downstream WLS state estimation.
Outputs: IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications, 2026 [J1]; NPSC 2024 [C3].
